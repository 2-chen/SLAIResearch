# 研究主题示例

启动 ChenResearch 后，直接输入以下任意主题即可开始全自动科研。Agent 会自动完成文献检索→实验设计→SCO 执行→论文撰写→审稿迭代。

## 原创研究 (Original Research)

直接输入主题，Agent 自动设计并执行实验：

- `A novel attention mechanism for long sequences in transformer models`
- `Improving few-shot learning through adaptive prompt optimization`
- `Federated learning with differential privacy for medical imaging`
- `Multi-agent reinforcement learning for robot collaboration in unstructured environments`
- `Contrastive learning for molecular property prediction`
- `Graph neural networks for drug-drug interaction prediction`
- `Self-supervised learning for time series anomaly detection`
- `Knowledge distillation from large language models to small task-specific models`
- `Adversarial robustness through randomized smoothing with adaptive variance`
- `Efficient video understanding via sparse temporal attention`

## 综述论文 (Survey Paper)

前缀 `survey:short:` / `survey:standard:` / `survey:long:` 控制篇幅和深度：

- `survey:short: LLM reasoning methods beyond chain-of-thought`
- `survey:short: Parameter-efficient fine-tuning for large language models`
- `survey:standard: Neural network pruning techniques from 2020-2026`
- `survey:standard: Diffusion models for molecular generation`
- `survey:standard: Tool-augmented language models — a comprehensive survey`
- `survey:long: Attention mechanisms — from self-attention to flash attention and beyond`
- `survey:long: Reinforcement learning from human feedback — methods, theory, and applications`

## 方法论研究 (Methodology Focus)

侧重算法设计和理论分析：

- `A theoretical analysis of grokking in small transformer models`
- `On the convergence properties of adaptive optimization methods`
- `Provable guarantees for differentially private stochastic gradient descent`
- `Information-theoretic bounds for meta-learning generalization`

## 实证研究 (Empirical Focus)

侧重实验评估和基准测试：

- `A large-scale empirical study of data augmentation techniques for NLP`
- `Benchmarking graph neural networks on molecular property prediction`
- `What matters in training vision transformers — an empirical investigation`
- `A systematic evaluation of retrieval-augmented generation strategies`

## 提示技巧

1. **越具体越好** — "Improving X through Y for Z application" 比 "research on AI" 好
2. **包含领域关键词** — 帮助文献检索精确命中目标论文
3. **可以迭代细化** — Agent 完成初步文献检索后会建议细化方向，你可以调整
